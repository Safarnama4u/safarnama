TRAVEL GUIDE • HIMACHAL PRADESH

Manali Travel Guide

Manali is one of India's popular mountain destinations, known for Himalayan views, valleys, rivers, adventure activities and beautiful surrounding nature.

📍 Location

Where is Manali?

Manali is located in Himachal Pradesh, in the Kullu Valley.

📅 Best Time

When to visit?

Different seasons offer different experiences, from pleasant summer trips to winter snow.

🚗 How to Reach

Getting there

Manali can be reached by road from major cities such as Chandigarh and Delhi. The nearest major airport is at Bhuntar.

💰 Budget

Estimated budget

Your total cost depends on your transport, hotel, food, activities and length of stay.


Things to See

Explore places around Manali such as Solang Valley, Old Manali, Hidimba Devi Temple, Vashisht and nearby Himalayan landscapes.

What to Carry

Carry comfortable shoes, suitable clothing, personal medicines, identification documents, water and basic travel essentials.

Travel Responsibly

Keep the mountains clean, avoid littering and respect local communities and the natural environment.
